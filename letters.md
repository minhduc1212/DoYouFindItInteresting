## 🛠️ TIPS FOR GETTING THE MOST OUT OF PAYWALLED SITES

- **Use a library card**: Many public libraries provide free access to NYT, The Atlantic, and academic databases.
- **12ft.io / Archive.ph**: Archived versions of articles are often freely accessible.
- **Google Scholar**: Search academic papers — many authors self-host PDFs legally.
- **Unpaywall browser extension**: Automatically finds free legal versions of academic papers.
- **RSS readers (Feedly, NewsBlur, Inoreader)**: Subscribe to dozens of free sites in one place.
- **Pocket / Instapaper**: Save articles to read later, offline, in a distraction-free format.
